# Version 1.1.0

The original 69 numbered statements are retained. Four new sections add
14 numbered statements with complete written proofs:

- A normalized symmetric return and its exact projective squareclass.
- Smooth plane-quartic pencils with a congruent-number elliptic factor
  and two isogenous factors that change under the common distance twist.
- A dyadic independence criterion and its uniform rank-at-least-two
  consequence for the original elliptic sources.
- Real bounds and exact valuations for all eight signed distance sums,
  the colour rule for nonsplit denominator primes, and the two product
  denominators.
- A three-pattern requirement when the radius content is one, a split-prime
  consequence for a single nontrivial corner colour, and a sharper dyadic bound.
- The complete rational-point sets of the one-fifth genus-three quotient
  and genus-five source, proved by an explicit height comparison and exact
  arithmetic certificates.

The Lean companion increases from 34 to 59 fully formalized statements of
the original manuscript. Against the expanded manuscript the coverage is
59/83. All 25 source projects were rebuilt and their audit endpoints were
cold-kernel checked. The verifier now recognizes both the standard Lean
axiom report and the tagged output of the native dependency audits.

The mathematical source files of the accepted Lean proofs are unchanged
from their verified development snapshot. Dependency versions and the
allowed axiom set remain pinned. No formalization credit is assigned to
the new paper statements or to supporting declarations separately.
